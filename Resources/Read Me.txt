ThreeFinger

Three-finger trackpad gestures for music and video:

  Tap with three fingers                  Play or pause
  Swipe up or down with three fingers     Volume up or down
  Swipe left or right with three fingers  Previous or next track


Installing

1. Drag ThreeFinger onto the Applications folder in this window.

2. Open ThreeFinger from Applications. Because it isn't from the App Store,
   macOS stops it the first time:
   - macOS 15 or later: click Done, then open System Settings >
     Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to
     ThreeFinger.
   - macOS 13 or 14: Control-click ThreeFinger, choose Open, then click Open.

3. When macOS asks, allow ThreeFinger in System Settings >
   Privacy & Security > Accessibility. It needs this to press the media keys.


Using it

Click the hand icon in the menu bar for settings. If a three-finger swipe also
opens Mission Control or drags windows, the panel shows which macOS setting
to change.

To hide the icon, turn off Show in menu bar. Opening ThreeFinger again brings
the panel back.

Needs macOS 13 or later.
